## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Micro-node density triad
The research network atmosphere SHALL render approximately three times as many nodes/particles as the pre-change counts, with node/star draw radii approximately one third of the pre-change sizes, so the field reads as a denser fine-grained dust rather than sparse large stars.

#### Scenario: More and smaller points
- **WHEN** the atmosphere is visible on Study entry or authentication
- **THEN** points appear markedly smaller and more numerous than before (about ×3 count and ÷3 size)

### Requirement: Triple concurrent connections
The atmosphere SHALL allow approximately three times as many live connecting edges at once as the pre-change maximum, so many short connections can appear concurrently without waiting for prior edges to fully fade.

#### Scenario: Many edges at once
- **WHEN** the atmosphere is animating with motion enabled
- **THEN** the viewer can perceive roughly triple the previous concurrent connection density (short near-neighbor edges)

### Requirement: Stronger central particle density
The atmosphere SHALL bias particle density toward the nucleus more strongly than the orbital rim, so the center feels denser with micro-points emerging and fading while outer rings remain comparatively lighter.

#### Scenario: Center denser than rim
- **WHEN** the atmosphere is visible
- **THEN** the core region shows higher micro-point density than the outer orbits
