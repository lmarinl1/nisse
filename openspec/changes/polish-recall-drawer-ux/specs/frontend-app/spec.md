## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Recuerdo drawers respect Research Drawer scroll and CTA polish
Create-recuerdo and edit-recuerdo surfaces in the Study Workspace SHALL use the Research Drawer behaviors for compact footer CTAs and scrollable body so long Recuerdo edits remain usable.

#### Scenario: Create and edit recuerdo usable
- **WHEN** the owner creates a recuerdo or edits an existing one in a drawer
- **THEN** the primary CTA is compact and the drawer body scrolls when content overflows
